---
title: "How to vote for where una go meet and choose restaurant"
description: "Check budget, how far each place dey, and whether space dey before you use the vote choose where to book."
---

Before una vote, agree on **how much each person fit spend and how many seats una need**. If person get food or access needs wey the place must meet, check am before you add the place.

## Give people the same kind of information for each place

When you create the event for Time2Gather, turn on location vote and add the restaurant names and branches. When you share the link, tell people things wey dem fit compare. For example, e take five minutes waka from station reach Restaurant A and ten minutes reach B.

## Choose place wey una fit book

Say A get six votes and B get four. If A no get seats for eight for the time wey una agree and B get, check whether B fit the budget and wetin una need. Book am, then share the branch name and address.
