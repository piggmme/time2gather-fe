---
title: "How to vote on a meeting place and choose a restaurant"
description: "Shortlist restaurants by budget, travel time, and group size, then use the poll results to choose a place you can book."
---

Before starting a venue poll, agree on **a budget per person and the number of seats you’ll need**. Check any dietary or accessibility needs that a venue must meet before adding it.

## Make the options easy to compare

Turn on location voting when you create an event in Time2Gather and add the restaurant names and branches. When you share the link, give the same details for each option, such as a five-minute walk from the station for Restaurant A and a ten-minute walk for Restaurant B.

## Pick an option you can book

Say Restaurant A gets six votes and Restaurant B gets four. If A can’t seat eight people at the agreed time but B can, check that B meets the budget and required conditions, then book it. Once you have a reservation, share the branch name and address.
