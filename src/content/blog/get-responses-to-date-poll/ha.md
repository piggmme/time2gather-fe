---
title: "Yadda ake samun amsoshin zaɓen ranar taro"
description: "Saita lokacin ƙarshe na amsawa kuma aika tunatarwa mai sauƙi ga ƙungiyar."
---

Lokacin ƙarshe yana nuna wa mahalarta lokacin amsawa kuma yana taimaka wa mai shiryawa ya tsara lokacin duba sakamako.

Aika tsawon lokacin taron da ake tsammani da lokacin ƙarshe tare da zaɓen. Misali: “Ku zaɓi ranakun da kuka samu kafin ƙarfe shida na yamma ranar Juma'a.”

Kafin lokaci ya ƙare, sake aika hanyar haɗi da lokacin ƙarshe ga waɗanda ba su amsa ba. Duba samuwar mutanen da dole su halarta, sannan a sanar da kowa ranar da aka zaɓa.
