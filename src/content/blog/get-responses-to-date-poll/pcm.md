---
title: "How to get answers for group date poll"
description: "Set deadline and send clear reminder make everybody choose when dem free."
---

Deadline tells participants when to reply and helps the organizer know when to check results.

Send expected meeting length and deadline with the poll. For example: “Abeg choose dates wey you free before 6 p.m. Friday.”

Before deadline, send the link and deadline again to people wey never reply. Check whether the people wey must attend dey free, choose the date and tell everybody.
