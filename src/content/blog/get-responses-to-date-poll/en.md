---
title: "How to Get Responses to a Group Date Poll"
description: "Set a response deadline and use a clear reminder to collect availability for your group meeting."
---

A response deadline tells participants when to answer and gives the organizer a time to review the results.

Share the expected meeting length and response deadline with the poll. For example: “Please choose the dates you can make by 6 p.m. Friday.”

Before the deadline, resend the meeting link and deadline to anyone who has not replied. Check the availability of essential attendees, choose a final date, and share it with everyone.
