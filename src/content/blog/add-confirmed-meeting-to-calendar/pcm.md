---
title: "How to put confirmed meeting time for your calendar"
description: "Export confirmed Time2Gather meeting as ICS calendar file, then check di date, time and time zone for your calendar app."
---

Even when everybody don agree on one time, chat message fit waka go far. Export the confirmed meeting from Time2Gather so e go dey with your other plans for calendar.

## Export the time wey don confirm

When the meeting host don confirm di date and time, open the meeting result page and tap the calendar button. Time2Gather go make one ICS calendar file. If una never confirm am, choose one available time for the export window.

## Check the event for your calendar app

ICS file fit open for common apps like Google Calendar and Apple Calendar. After you add am, check the meeting title, start and finish time, and time zone. If participants dey different places, make everybody check the local time wey their own calendar show.

## Share the meeting link separately

The ICS file save one event for calendar; e no invite people or update later changes by itself. Share the Time2Gather meeting link from the result page too. If the date change, update the calendar event or add the file again.
