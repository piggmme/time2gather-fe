---
title: "How to Add a Confirmed Meeting to Your Calendar"
description: "Export a confirmed Time2Gather meeting as an ICS calendar file, then check the date, time, and time zone in your calendar app."
---

Once a group agrees on a time, a chat message can be easy to miss. Exporting the confirmed meeting from Time2Gather gives you a calendar event you can check alongside your other plans.

## Export the confirmed time

When the host confirms the date and time, open the meeting results page and select the calendar button. Time2Gather creates an ICS calendar file. If the meeting is not confirmed yet, choose a time from the available options in the export window.

## Check the event in your calendar app

ICS files can be opened in common calendar apps, including Google Calendar and Apple Calendar. After importing the file, check the meeting title, start and end times, and time zone. If attendees live in different regions, each person should check the local time shown by their own calendar.

## Share the meeting link separately

The ICS file saves an event to a calendar; it does not invite attendees or automatically sync later changes. Share the Time2Gather meeting link from the results page so everyone can revisit the schedule. If the date changes, update or import the calendar event again.
