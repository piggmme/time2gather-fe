---
title: "How to find a time that works for a group"
description: "Use an availability poll to compare who can make the start time and who can stay for the whole meeting."
---

Before sending a poll, decide **how long you need** and whether anyone has to be there. For a one-hour meeting, check who can stay for the full hour as well as who can make the start time.

## Ask people to mark every time they can make

Set the dates and time ranges in Time2Gather and share the link. For example, offer Friday evening and Saturday afternoon. Ask participants to mark all the time they’re free. If someone can make only Friday from 6 to 6:30 p.m., they can mark just that half hour.

## Compare who can start and who can stay

Suppose five people can start Friday at 6 p.m., but only three can stay until 7 p.m. Four people, including the facilitator, can make the full hour from 2 to 3 p.m. on Saturday. If you need an hour together, pick Saturday. Use the calendar in the results to check when required participants are free and make sure the same people are available through the end.
