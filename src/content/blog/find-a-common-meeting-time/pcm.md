---
title: "How to find time wey plenty people fit meet"
description: "Use time poll check who fit show up when meeting start and who fit stay reach the end."
---

Before you send poll, decide **how long the meeting go take** and who need to dey there. If una need one hour, check who fit stay for the whole hour as well as who fit reach when una start.

## Tell everybody make dem mark all their free time

Choose dates and time ranges for Time2Gather and share the link. You fit offer Friday evening and Saturday afternoon. Tell people make dem mark all the times wey dem free. If person free only Friday from 6 to 6:30 p.m., make dem mark just that half hour.

## Compare who fit start with who fit stay

Say five people fit come by 6 p.m. on Friday, but only three fit stay till 7 p.m. On Saturday, four people fit stay from 2 to 3 p.m., including the person wey go lead the meeting. If una need the full hour together, choose Saturday. For the results calendar, check when the people wey must attend dey free and confirm say na the same people fit stay reach the end.
