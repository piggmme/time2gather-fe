title: "Wetin to send after una confirm meeting time"
description: "Share the date, place and wetin people need to bring inside one clear message."
---

Once una confirm the time, send the date, start time, time zone and meeting place or call link together. For example: “Make we meet Saturday December 5 by 2 p.m. for front of Seoul Station exit 2.”

Add when una expect to finish and wetin people suppose bring. If you book place, check the branch and address. If plans change, share the new details and meeting link again.
