---
title: "What to Send After Confirming a Meeting"
description: "Share the confirmed date, location and preparation details in one clear message."
---

Once the time is confirmed, send the date, start time, time zone, and venue or call link together. For example: “Meet at 2 p.m. Saturday, December 5, outside Seoul Station exit 2.”

Add the expected end time and anything people should bring. If you made a reservation, check the branch and address. If plans change, share the updated details and meeting link again.
