---
title: "How to choose regular time for weekly study group"
description: "Check the real dates for the next four weeks to see which day enough people fit show up steady."
---

For weekly study group, check **whether enough people fit meet on the same day every week**. First agree how long each session go take and how many people una need to start. For example, group of six fit agree to study for one hour any time at least five people fit join.

## Put the real dates for the next four weeks inside the poll

If Tuesday and Thursday evening fit work, put all the Tuesdays and Thursdays for the next four weeks inside a Time2Gather time poll. Ask people make dem mark each date wey dem fit stay the whole time, like 8 to 9 p.m. That way work shifts and plans wey dem already get go show.

## Choose the day wey enough people fit come for more weeks

Say at least five people fit come all four Tuesdays. All six fit come the first Thursday, but people wey fit come each of the next three Thursdays no reach five. Choose Tuesday as una regular study day. When you tell everybody the schedule, add whether una go skip or move a session when people no reach.
