---
title: "How to pick a weekly study group time that sticks"
description: "Poll real dates over the next four weeks to find the weekday when enough people can meet consistently."
---

For a weekly study group, check **how reliably enough people can meet on the same weekday**. Decide how long each session lasts and how many people you need to go ahead. For example, a group of six might agree to study for an hour whenever at least five people can join.

## Poll the actual dates for the next four weeks

If Tuesday and Thursday evenings are options, add the actual Tuesdays and Thursdays for the next four weeks to a Time2Gather time poll. Ask people to mark whether they can make the full slot, say 8–9 p.m., on each date. This captures shift changes and prior commitments.

## Choose the weekday that meets your minimum more often

Suppose at least five people can make Tuesday in all four weeks. All six can make Thursday in week one, but fewer than five can make it in each of the other three weeks. Set your weekly session for Tuesday. When you announce the schedule, say whether you’ll skip or reschedule a week when too few people can make it.
