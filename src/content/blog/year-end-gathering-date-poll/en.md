---
title: "How to pick a date for a year-end get-together"
description: "Shortlist realistic dates, share a scheduling poll, and use this copy-and-paste message to collect replies in your group chat."
---

Start with three to five realistic dates. Ask everyone to mark **every date they can make**. If each person picks just one favorite, you won't know how many could also come on a date they ranked second.

## A message to send with your date poll

> I'm planning a dinner get-together in December. Please use the link below to mark every date you can make by Wednesday at 9 p.m. I'll check the replies and confirm the date on Thursday.

Paste the poll link below the message. If people finish work at different times, include a realistic evening window so they can mark times such as 6:30 or 7:30 p.m.

## How to choose from the results

Start with the slot the most people can make. If six people can make Friday at 6:30 p.m. and six can make Saturday at 7:30 p.m., check which option would have fewer late arrivals. If someone needs to be there, check their availability before you confirm.
