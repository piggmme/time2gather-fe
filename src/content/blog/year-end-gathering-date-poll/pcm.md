---
title: "How to choose date for end-of-year hangout"
description: "Pick some dates wey fit work, then copy dis message send with your poll link for di group chat."
---

Start with three to five dates wey make sense. Tell everybody make dem mark **all di dates wey dem fit come**. If each person vote only for di day wey dem like pass, you no go know how many people fit still show face anoda day.

## Message wey you fit send for di group

> Make we chop dinner together for December? Abeg use dis link mark all di dates wey you fit come before Wednesday 9 p.m. I go check who fit come and confirm di date on Thursday.

Put di poll link under di message. If people dey close from work at different times, give dem some choices for evening so dem fit mark time like 6:30 or 7:30 p.m.

## How to choose after people don vote

First check di time wey most people fit come. If six people fit come Friday 6:30 p.m. and six fit come Saturday 7:30 p.m., check which one go make fewer people reach late. If e get person wey must dey there, check say di person fit come before you confirm.

For Time2Gather, you fit choose dates and set di evening time range, then share di invite link. Your friends fit put their names and mark when dem free without signing in.
