---
title: "How to pick a date for a team dinner"
description: "Run a date poll and compare overall turnout with the availability of anyone the team dinner is for."
---

Decide **who needs to be there** before you send the poll. For a welcome dinner, start with dates that work for the new teammate. For a casual team dinner, the date most people can make is a reasonable choice.

## Include the start time when you share the poll

If you have already agreed on 7 p.m., create a date-only poll in Time2Gather. Include the start time and response deadline when you share the link.

> The welcome dinner will start at 7 p.m. Please use the link to mark every date you can make. Reply by 5 p.m. on Friday so we can confirm the date.

## Compare turnout on the dates that work for required guests

Suppose eight of ten people can make Monday and six can make Wednesday. If the new teammate can only make Wednesday, pick Wednesday. If they can make both dates, pick Monday. Check directly with anyone who needs to attend and has yet to reply by the deadline.
