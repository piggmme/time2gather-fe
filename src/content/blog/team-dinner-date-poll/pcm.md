---
title: "How to choose date for team dinner"
description: "Check how many people fit come and which days work for anybody wey need to dey there."
---

Before you send the poll, decide **who need to dey there**. If na welcome dinner for new teammate, start with the days wey the person fit come. For normal team dinner, you fit choose the day wey most people fit come.

## Put the start time for the message

If una don agree to start by 7 p.m., use Time2Gather make poll wey ask for dates only. When you share the link, add the start time and when people suppose reply.

> The welcome dinner go start by 7 p.m. Abeg open the link and mark every day wey you fit come. Reply before 5 p.m. on Friday so we fit confirm the date.

## Compare the days wey work for people wey must come

Say eight out of ten fit come on Monday and six fit come on Wednesday. If the new teammate fit come only on Wednesday, choose Wednesday. If both days work for the person, choose Monday. If person wey need to dey there never reply by the deadline, ask dem directly before you confirm.
