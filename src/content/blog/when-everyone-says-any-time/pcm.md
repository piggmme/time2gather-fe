title: "When everybody talk say “any time dey okay”: how to pick meeting time"
description: "Give clear time options and make am easy for friends to say no."
---

“Any time dey okay for me.” Imagine say three friends reply like that. One person open calendar but still no know when the others really free. This na imaginary group chat, but a few simple steps fit help una move forward.

## Give options wey easy to answer

Instead of asking “when una free?”, mention the date, start time and how long una plan to meet. For example: “Una fit do two hours from 2 p.m. Saturday or from 11 a.m. Sunday?” If person dey come from far, add the place or area too.

## Make “both no work” a valid answer

Tell people say dem fit suggest another time or no attend if both options hard. Ask people wey must attend first, and find new options if none work. Nobody needs pressure to explain why dem no fit come.

## Decide and share the plan

Say when you need replies, then send the chosen time and place. For example: “Abeg tell me before Thursday evening; if both no work, talk am freely!” Clear options and freedom to decline help pass guessing wetin everybody mean by ‘any time’.
