---
title: "How to Schedule a Meeting Across Time Zones"
description: "Set a reference time zone in Time2Gather, let each participant view local meeting times, and check date changes before confirming."
---

A time that is 10 a.m. in Seoul can fall on a different date or hour in London or New York. Set a clear reference time zone for the poll, then let each participant view the schedule in their own region to avoid confusion.

## Choose one reference time zone

When creating a timed meeting in Time2Gather, select the time zone for the place hosting the meeting. In your invitation, write something like, “The proposed time is 10 a.m. Seoul time.” Naming the city makes the reference clear to everyone.

## View the poll in each participant’s local time

On the availability or results page, participants can use the display time zone selector to choose their city. Time2Gather converts the meeting times for that view, so no one has to calculate UTC offsets by hand. Check whether a converted time falls on the previous or next day.

## Recheck the date before confirming

Choose a slot that works for the people who need to attend. Before confirming, the host should check the reference zone and the local dates participants will see. For a meeting near a daylight saving change, select a named city such as Seoul or London instead of relying on a fixed numeric offset.
