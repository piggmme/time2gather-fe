---
title: "How to plan meeting time for people wey dey different time zones"
description: "Choose one reference time zone for Time2Gather, make everybody see their local time, and check the date before una confirm."
---

10 a.m. for Seoul fit be another time, or even the day before, for London or New York. Choose one clear time zone for the poll, then make each person check the time for their own area.

## Choose one time zone for the meeting

When you dey create meeting wey get time options for Time2Gather, choose the time zone for where the meeting go happen. For the invite, write something like, “The time we dey suggest na 10 a.m. Seoul time.” City name go make the time clear for everybody.

## Make each person see their own local time

For the availability or results page, each person fit choose their city for the display time zone picker. Time2Gather go convert the time, so nobody need calculate UTC difference by hand. Check if the date don move to the day before or after.

## Check the date again before you confirm

Choose time wey the people wey must attend fit make. Before confirmation, the host suppose check the main time zone and the local dates everybody go see. If the meeting near daylight saving change, choose city like Seoul or London instead of one fixed number offset.
